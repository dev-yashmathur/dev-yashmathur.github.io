Privacy

No analytics, no tracking, no network requests. ShortsGone asks Chrome for three things, and here is what each one is for.

https://www.youtube.com/*

youtube.com only

The one site ShortsGone can run on. It adds a few hiding rules to YouTube’s pages and nothing anywhere else.

storage

Remembers four things: the main switch and the three toggles. They are saved in Chrome on your computer.

Questions

How do I block YouTube Shorts in Chrome?

Add ShortsGone from the Chrome Web Store. It is on from the moment it is installed: Shorts leave the home feed, sidebar, search results and subscriptions, and /shorts/ links are blocked. There is nothing to set up.

Is ShortsGone free?

Yes. ShortsGone is free on the Chrome Web Store. There is no account to create and nothing to upgrade to.

Can I hide Shorts in some places but not others?

Yes. Open the popup and expand Advanced Settings. There are three toggles: Sidebar Navigation, Home & Subscription Feeds, and Search Results. Turn off any of them and Shorts come back in just that place.

Can I turn the Shorts blocker off for a while?

Yes. Click the power button in the popup. The badge switches from Shorts Blocked to Shorts Allowed and Shorts reappear. Click it again to block them.

What happens when I open a YouTube Shorts link?

While blocking is on, any youtube.com/shorts/ address, whether you open it directly or click into it inside YouTube, is replaced by a “YouTube Shorts Blocked” page with a button back to YouTube.

Will I see Shorts flash before they disappear?

No. ShortsGone adds its hiding rules as the page starts loading, before YouTube draws anything, so Shorts are hidden instead of popping in and then vanishing.

Does ShortsGone collect my data or browsing history?

No. Your switch and toggle settings are saved in Chrome’s local extension storage on your computer. There are no analytics, no tracking and no network requests, and the extension only runs on youtube.com.

Does it work on my phone or in the YouTube app?

No. ShortsGone is a Chrome extension, so it works on youtube.com in Chrome on your computer. It cannot change the YouTube app on a phone.

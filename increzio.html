Most habit apps are either strict checklists or punishing streak counters. I wanted something friendlier: my streak on the home screen, goals that grow a little at a time, and a day that ends when I go to sleep.

Home screen widgets

A habit tracker that lives on your home screen

I loved streaks, but only remembered them when I opened the app. So Increzio puts them where you look a hundred times a day. Tick a habit right from the widget and the streak updates without opening anything.

  • A streak widget with your current run
  • A Today widget you can tick habits on
  • Tap through to the app for the rest

Tiny gains, big results

Incremental goals that grow 1% at a time

Inspired by Atomic Habits: tiny daily improvements compound. For anything you measure, set a starting goal, a target and a percentage. Every time you complete the habit, the goal nudges up, until it reaches your target.

  • Pages, minutes, kilometres, any unit
  • Grows 1% by default, or whatever you choose
  • A chart shows the goal climbing as you go

For night owls

A habit tracker with a custom day reset

I’m a night owl. Every tracker I tried decided my day was over at midnight, while I was still doing the habits. In Increzio you choose when your new day starts, so a late-night tick counts for the day you were actually living.

  • Start your day anywhere from 12 AM to 12 PM
  • Streaks and “today” follow your reset time
  • An optional nudge an hour before the reset, only if habits are still open

Streaks break. Habits don’t.

A streak tracker that forgives one honest slip

Did the habit, forgot to tick it? A broken streak is when a lot of people give up, so Increzio offers a Streak Shield instead: three a month, to save yesterday. Count your streak by all habits or by any habit, whichever keeps you going.

  • Three Streak Shields every month
  • “All habits” or “Any habit” streaks
  • Milestones from your first step to a full year

Analytics

See the week, the month and the climb

A weekly recap shows your check-ins day by day, your best day and your most consistent habit. Switch to a month or three, and every measurable habit gets its own progress chart.

  • Week, month and 3-month views
  • Best day and most consistent habit
  • Progress charts for habits you measure

Paper and ink

Hand-drawn on purpose, in light or dark

I sketched Increzio away from the over-polished look of most apps: ink outlines, pastel fills and a little logo that breathes, dozes late in the evening and bounces when your day is done. Pick light, dark or follow your phone.

Questions

Is Increzio free?

Yes. Increzio is free on Google Play. There is no subscription and no premium tier.

Does Increzio have home screen widgets?

Yes, two. A streak widget keeps your current streak on your home screen, and the Today widget lists your habits for the day so you can tick them off without opening the app.

Can I change when my habit day resets?

Yes. In Settings, pick when your new day starts, anywhere from 12 AM to 12 PM. Set it to 4 AM and a habit you tick at 1:47 AM still counts for the day you were living. You can also get a gentle reminder an hour before the reset if habits are still open.

What are incremental (progressive) goals?

For habits you measure, like pages or kilometres, you set a starting goal, a target and a percentage (1% by default). Each time you complete the habit, the goal grows by that percentage until it reaches your target.

What happens to my streak if I miss a day?

If you did the habit but forgot to tick it, a Streak Shield can save yesterday. You get three a month. You can also count your streak by all habits or by any habit.

Is Increzio based on Atomic Habits?

It is inspired by the book’s idea that tiny daily improvements compound over time. That is where the 1% goals come from. The app is not affiliated with the book.

Do I need an account? Where is my data stored?

No account. Your habits live on your phone. If you want a backup, you can optionally sign in with Google to keep a copy in Google Drive.

Is Increzio available on iPhone?

Right now Increzio is available for Android on Google Play.

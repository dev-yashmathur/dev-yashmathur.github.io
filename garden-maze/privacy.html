Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

The Garden Maze (the first-person maze game, also known as FirstPersonMaze) does not collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no tracking and no ads. You walk the mazes, and what happens in them stays on your device.

Data We Collect

None. Specifically, The Garden Maze:

  • Does not collect, store or transmit any personal information to us or to anyone else.
  • Does not require an account, sign-in or email address.
  • Does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.
  • Does not send gameplay data, device identifiers or usage statistics off your device.
  • Plays fully offline.

What Stays on Your Device

To remember your progress between sessions, the game saves a small amount of information locally on your device: the coins you have collected, the themes you have unlocked, your hints, and your best coin counts on each level. This information is never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the game removes it.

Third-Party Services

The game does not include analytics, crash reporting, advertising networks, social sign-in or any other third-party tracking tools, and it does not offer real-money purchases. Coins are earned in play and spent in the in-game shop only.

Children

Because the game collects no data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children either.

Changes to This Policy

If a future version adds anything that changes this, such as online features, this policy will be updated and the change clearly described before that version is released.

Questions

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please reach out through the contact links on the main portfolio site.

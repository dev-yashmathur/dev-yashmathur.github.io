Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

ShortsGone is built with strict privacy and security standards. The extension operates entirely on your local machine with zero external communication, no remote code, and zero data collection.

Zero Data Collection & Storage

We do not collect, store, track, or transmit any user data or browsing history.

  • No Data Collection: We do not log your searches, viewing history, account details, or personal information.
  • 100% Local Execution: Nothing ever leaves your computer or browser.
  • Local Preferences Only: Your display preferences are stored strictly inside your browser using Chrome's local storage (chrome.storage.local).

How the Extension Works

ShortsGone operates via a fixed, pre-compiled stylesheet injection directly at the client end:

  • It injects lightweight CSS rules into YouTube pages locally to hide distracting Shorts elements from view.
  • There is no remote code execution, no external scripts loaded dynamically, and no arbitrary code evaluation.
  • There is no data processing, machine learning, or background scraping of any kind.

No Third-Party Services & No Network Requests

ShortsGone makes zero network requests. It contains no third-party analytics, no tracking pixels, no telemetry, and no advertising libraries.

Permissions Justification

ShortsGone requests the absolute minimum permissions necessary to perform its single purpose:

  • storage: Used exclusively to save your UI preferences locally on your device.
  • webNavigation: Used strictly to detect when a /shorts/ URL is requested and route you away from the distraction locally.
  • youtube.com host permission: Required to inject the local stylesheet onto YouTube pages.
